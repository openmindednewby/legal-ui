Top fixes:
1. Give the padded area the screen's own background: in `ConsentAwareStack` set
   `contentStyle: { paddingBottom: bottomInset, backgroundColor: <theme screen background> }` (white on erevna),
   so the strip behind the banner is the same surface as the screen. Better in legal-ui: export a
   `consentInsetStyle(background)` helper so every portal gets it right on adoption.
2. Banner card bottom padding grew: Privacy Policy to card edge is ~30px at 360 (before ~16px) and the card is
   ~24px taller at 1280 (598-783 vs 622-783), with no change above. Make the bottom padding equal the top
   padding again (cause not traced; likely the onLayout wrapper or a doubled inset).
